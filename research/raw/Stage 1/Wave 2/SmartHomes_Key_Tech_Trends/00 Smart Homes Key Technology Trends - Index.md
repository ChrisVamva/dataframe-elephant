# Smart Homes: Key Technology Trends

> **Vibe:** The smart home is moving from a collection of gadgets toward an interoperable, AI-assisted, energy-aware system.

## Research questions

- Does standardization expand the market faster than it reduces differentiation?
- Which AI automations deliver measurable value without creating unacceptable privacy or safety risk?
- Can connected homes become reliable participants in demand response and distributed energy systems?
- Which vendors can support products securely over their full useful life?

## How to use this vault

Start with the architecture map, then follow links from each note to sources and open questions. The original prompts remain in `Research Orientation/` and are treated as research hypotheses, not established facts.
