## Evidence discipline

- **Documented fact:** directly supported by a linked source.
- **Synthesis:** interpretation across sources, labeled by wording such as “suggests” or “is shifting.”
- **Recommendation:** design or evaluation guidance, not a claim about market reality.
- Vendor announcements are treated as capability claims, not independent validation.
