# Emerging Product Categories and Platform Shifts

> **Vibe:** The next category winners may be the systems that coordinate devices, not the devices that add one more isolated function.

## Categories to track

- AI-powered hubs and home routers with Thread border-router functions.
- Voice-only and multimodal interfaces that create routines from descriptions.
- Energy monitors, home energy management systems, batteries, EV chargers, and flexible loads.
- AI-assisted security cameras, doorbells, sensors, and professional monitoring.
- Automated comfort systems combining HVAC, lighting, occupancy, and weather.

## Why these categories matter

They sit at control points: identity, network access, household context, energy optimization, or safety response. Their defensibility may come from reliable orchestration, data governance, installation/support, and ecosystem relationships rather than a single sensor feature.

## Evaluation checklist

1. Is the product useful without a paid cloud subscription?
2. Does it interoperate through Matter or a clearly documented API?
3. What happens during internet, power, or vendor-service failure?
4. Can users export data, replace the hub, or migrate routines?
5. Is the value measurable in comfort, safety, time, or energy cost?

## Synthesis

The emerging product is increasingly a service layer: sensing plus interpretation plus bounded automation. This creates opportunities for subscription revenue, but also raises switching costs, privacy obligations, and customer-support expectations.
