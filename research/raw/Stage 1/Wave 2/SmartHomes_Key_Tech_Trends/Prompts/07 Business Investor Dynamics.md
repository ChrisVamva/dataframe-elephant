# Research Prompt: Business and Investor Dynamics

Investigate where durable competitive advantage and economic value remain after Matter reduces basic interoperability friction.

Answer:
- Which advantages survive standardization?
- Which recurring-revenue models retain customers without harmful lock-in?
- How do support, cybersecurity, warranty, and update obligations affect lifetime margins?
- Which companies benefit from smart-home and residential-energy convergence?
- Are device counts, active homes, engagement, automation execution, savings, and subscription revenue measured consistently?
- How will cyber-resilience regulation affect cost and market access?

Compare hardware, platform, subscription, installer, utility, insurance, and monitoring models. Use filings, unit economics, customer metrics, regulatory documents, and independent market evidence.
