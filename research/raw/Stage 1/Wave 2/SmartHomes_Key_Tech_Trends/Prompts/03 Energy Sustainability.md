# Research Prompt: Energy and Sustainability

Investigate verified energy, demand-response, comfort, equipment-life, and emissions outcomes from coordinated smart-home systems.

Compare HVAC, heat pumps, water heaters, batteries, solar, EV charging, and flexible appliances across climates, building types, tariffs, and control strategies.

Answer:
- What savings are measured rather than claimed?
- Which device combinations provide the greatest flexibility?
- What comfort or equipment-life penalties occur?
- Which tariffs, aggregators, and regulations make flexibility financially attractive?
- Can Matter-based controls interoperate with utilities and home-energy-management systems?

Prefer field trials, utility data, government or laboratory studies, and transparent models. State assumptions, baselines, uncertainty, and who receives the value.
