# Research Prompt: Matter, Thread, and Interoperability

Investigate the real-world state of Matter 1.5/1.6 and Thread interoperability across Apple, Google, Amazon, and Samsung ecosystems.

Answer:
- Which device types and features work consistently?
- How often do optional features, extensions, or certification gaps cause incompatibility?
- What fails when border routers, home routers, or cloud services go offline?
- Can users migrate devices, routines, and permissions without rebuilding?
- How do Matter and Thread compare with Zigbee, Z-Wave, and proprietary systems over 5–10 years?

Compare commissioning, multi-admin, updates, local control, failure recovery, and migration. Use standards documentation, certification records, independent tests, and user evidence. End with an interoperability risk assessment and product-design implications.
