# Research Prompt: Security, Privacy, and Lifecycle

Investigate the minimum trustworthy lifecycle for smart locks, cameras, hubs, routers, appliances, energy devices, and AI assistants.

Answer:
- What support periods and update mechanisms should consumers expect?
- How effective are segmentation, automatic updates, MFA, and secure commissioning?
- Which data types create the greatest privacy risk?
- What happens when a cloud service shuts down or ownership changes?
- How should systems address prompt injection, unauthorized actions, biometric misuse, and surveillance?
- Which labels, certifications, and regulations provide meaningful assurance?

Compare NIST, FTC, EU, industry standards, vulnerability disclosures, and vendor policies. Produce a lifecycle control checklist and identify gaps between formal compliance and practical household protection.
