# Research Prompt: AI and Contextual Home Control

Investigate the accuracy, safety, privacy, and usability of AI-generated automations, camera summaries, and natural-language smart-home control.

Answer:
- Which actions need confirmation versus safe autonomy?
- How are ambiguity, household identities, guests, children, and adversarial voice commands handled?
- Which functions run locally, and what are the latency, cost, privacy, and capability trade-offs?
- Are generated routines understandable, editable, testable, and portable?

Compare major assistants using published evaluations, product documentation, controlled tests, and independent reviews. Measure or report error types, false positives, recovery behavior, permissions, and auditability. Distinguish roadmap claims from available functionality.
