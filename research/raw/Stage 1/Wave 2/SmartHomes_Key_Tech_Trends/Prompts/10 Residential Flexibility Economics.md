# Research Package Prompt: Residential Flexibility Economics

Model coordinated HVAC, heat-pump, water-heater, EV, battery, solar, and appliance control under multiple tariffs and climates.

Define baseline consumption, comfort bounds, equipment constraints, participation rates, incentives, aggregator fees, installation costs, maintenance, degradation, and rebound effects.

Calculate customer bill impact, utility value, peak reduction, emissions impact, payback, net present value, and distribution of benefits. Test outages, extreme weather, low participation, forecast error, and customer overrides.

Deliver assumptions, sensitivity analysis, customer/utility/aggregator value split, and the conditions under which automation is economically and socially worthwhile.
