# Research Prompt: Emerging Smart-Home Product Categories

Assess whether AI hubs, home routers, energy controllers, sensors, AI cameras, and automated comfort systems are durable categories or temporary feature bundles.

For each category, investigate:
- User problem and measurable outcome.
- Installation, maintenance, subscription, and replacement costs.
- Interoperability and local-control support.
- Failure behavior and vendor dependence.
- Defensibility after Matter standardization.
- Evidence of adoption, retention, and willingness to pay.

Identify categories created by responsible combination of occupancy, energy, appliance, and security data. Separate market evidence from vendor forecasts and conclude with a prioritized opportunity/risk matrix.
