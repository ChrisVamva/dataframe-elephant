# Research Prompt: Unresolved Tensions and Trade-offs

Investigate the unresolved tensions running across the smart-home architecture.

Analyze:
- Interoperability versus differentiation and ecosystem lock-in.
- AI convenience versus explainability, privacy, safety, and user control.
- Cloud intelligence versus local resilience, cost, latency, and privacy.
- Energy optimization versus comfort, equipment life, equity, and customer override.
- Continuous sensing versus household privacy and consent.
- Long-lived appliances versus short software and subscription lifecycles.
- Open standards versus vendor extensions and uneven implementation.
- Automation value versus setup complexity, failure recovery, and support burden.
- Recurring revenue versus affordability, portability, and trust.
- Security compliance versus measurable real-world protection.

For each tension, identify stakeholders, measurable variables, competing evidence, boundary conditions, worst-case failure, mitigation options, and what evidence would change the conclusion. End with a decision matrix showing when each side of the trade-off is preferable.
