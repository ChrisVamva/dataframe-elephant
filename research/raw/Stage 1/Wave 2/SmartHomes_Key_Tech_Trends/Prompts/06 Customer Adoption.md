# Research Prompt: Customer Adoption and Value

Investigate post-purchase adoption barriers and willingness to pay for smart-home systems.

Answer:
- Which barriers dominate: installation, configuration, maintenance, privacy, reliability, or subscriptions?
- Which outcomes justify payment: convenience, safety, comfort, energy savings, aging in place, or insurance benefits?
- How much setup can installers, retailers, utilities, or AI assistants handle without reducing user understanding?
- How do households calculate total cost of ownership?

Segment findings by housing type, technical confidence, household composition, income, climate, and ownership/rental status. Use surveys, interviews, behavioral data, and churn evidence. Report sample limits and distinguish stated preferences from observed behavior.
