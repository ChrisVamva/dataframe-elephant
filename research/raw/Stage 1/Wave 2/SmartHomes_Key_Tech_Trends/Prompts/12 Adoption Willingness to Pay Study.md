# Research Package Prompt: Adoption and Willingness-to-Pay Study

Design a mixed-method study of smart-home adoption, retention, and willingness to pay.

Segment participants by housing type, tenure, climate, household composition, technical confidence, income, energy burden, privacy preference, and safety needs.

Measure current device use, setup effort, failure experiences, trust, desired outcomes, subscription tolerance, total cost of ownership, and willingness to delegate decisions to AI. Combine stated-preference questions with observed behavior or usage evidence where possible.

Deliver sampling plan, interview guide, survey instrument, conjoint or pricing design, analysis plan, bias limitations, segments, adoption barriers, and product implications.
