# Laundering Cases — Documented Origin Failures and Enforcement

_Covers RQ2. Every claim cites `[S##]`. Each fact is labelled: **DOCUMENTED FACT** (enforcement/court/official data), **REPORTED SIGNAL** (journalistic investigation), or **ESTIMATE** (analyst modeling). Research pass of 2026-10-06._

## 1. Russian gold after the 2022 G7 bans

- **DOCUMENTED FACT (customs records):** Reuters's analysis of ~1,000 shipment records (Feb 24 2022–Mar 3 2023): the UAE imported **75.7 t of Russian gold worth $4.3B** (vs 1.3 t in all of 2021); UAE + China + Turkey = **99.8% of Russian gold exports**; 100+ named non-sanctioned buyers including traders and refiners [S24].
- **REPORTED SIGNAL:** The Insider found no Russian gold officially exported outside the EAEU after Apr 2023, while Armenia's gold import/export magnitudes rival its GDP — implying re-export with re-labelled origin through EAEU intermediaries [S35].
- **Mechanism note (inference):** G7 buyers' ban made Russian-origin gold unsellable *as Russian*; intermediary jurisdictions purchased legally, and re-refining/re-export re-papers origin for entry back into Western supply chains. Refinery pools make the re-papering unfalsifiable (see §6 and [S40]).

## 2. Russian diamonds

- **DOCUMENTED FACT:** G7/EU ban phases — direct import ban from Jan 1 2024; extended Mar 1 2024 to third-country-processed diamonds ≥1.0 ct; threshold lowered to ≥0.5 ct Sep 1 2024, with certification/traceability requirements; OFAC's FAQ applies "substantially transformed" language [S25].
- **REPORTED SIGNAL:** laundering concerns center on UAE (Dubai) and India re-papering; India polishes ~90% of the world's polished diamonds and lobbied for delays; the Antwerp node binds certificates only for the covered size band [S25].
- **Scale context:** ALROSA ~90% of Russian mining; Russia ~30% of global rough production by volume [S25].

## 3. Critical-minerals smuggling under China's own controls

- **DOCUMENTED FACT (official campaign):** China intensified a strategic-minerals anti-smuggling campaign (inter-agency, from a May 9 2025 Shenzhen meeting); Xinhua publicized "typical cases" of companies illegally exporting rare earths obtained through illegitimate channels (Oct 11 2025); state media report >36,000 t of illegal rare-earth products historically seized [S32].
- **REPORTED SIGNAL:** Ga/Ge transshipment via third countries to evade the Dec 2024 US-specific ban is reported in trade press, but no named enforcement case with quantities surfaced this session (open item).
- **REPORTED SIGNAL:** post-Nov-2025 general licenses, magnet exports surged (+13% overall; Europe +60%) — licensed expansion that also creates re-export cover [S60].
- **Irony noted for articles:** the exporter whose controls triggered the West's origin-integrity debate runs the largest anti-smuggling campaign of its own — because its controls created the arbitrage [S07, S32].

## 4. 3TG and cobalt re-papering

- **DOCUMENTED FACT (UN Group of Experts):** ≥**150 t of coltan** fraudulently exported from M23-held DRC territory **to Rwanda in 2024**; the Jul 3 2025 GoE report ties major Rwandan exporter Boss Mining Solution to smuggled minerals; Rwanda's mineral exports exceed $1B/yr while declared coltan volumes cannot be explained by domestic output [S26].
- **DOCUMENTED FACT (scheme self-report):** ITSCI traceability suspended in Masisi (N. Kivu) for almost all of 2024 (~120 t/month untraced); Myanmar 3T coverage effectively collapsed post-2021 coup [S27].
- **ESTIMATE (peer-reviewed/modeling):** artisanal cobalt (~20–30% of DRC output, 10–15% of global supply) is blended into industrial feeds; buyer attempts to separate ASM from industrial cobalt are "futile" [S28].
- **REPORTED SIGNAL:** Myanmar's Man Maw (Wa State) tin restarted 2025; Thai customs import surges used by researchers as a smuggling proxy [S27].

## 5. Chips and tooling: transshipment, shell buyers, prosecutions

- **DOCUMENTED FACT (court charges):** Singapore, Feb 27 2025 — three men charged with defrauding server suppliers (Dell, Super Micro) by falsely claiming servers would stay in Singapore; ministerial statement (Jul 7 2025) that seized servers may have contained Nvidia advanced AI chips; media link to DeepSeek procurement via Malaysia/Singapore intermediaries; Apr 2026 additional charge; Jul 2026 $42M mansion seized [S29].
- **DOCUMENTED FACT (customs action):** Malaysian customs foiled a ~US$13M AI-chip smuggling attempt at KLIA (Jun 2026) [S33].
- **DOCUMENTED FACT (indictments):** DOJ Nov 2025 — four defendants, ~400 Nvidia A100s to China via Malaysia/Thailand (Oct 2024–Jan 2025); ALX Solutions employees arrested Aug 2025; Cadence Jul 2025 resolution over sales to a Chinese military university [S33].
- **ESTIMATE (academic preprint):** 100,000–1,000,000 H100-class GPUs smuggled into the PRC post-controls [S34]; C4ADS identified 50 restricted-GPU export shipments routed via Vietnam/India/Malaysia to HK/China [S30].
- **REPORTED SIGNAL:** US embedded location trackers in AI-chip shipments after a 2024 diversion case [S13].

## 6. Solar / polysilicon / LFP circumvention

- **DOCUMENTED FACT (Commerce determinations):** final affirmative circumvention findings (Aug 18 2023, FR Aug 23 2023) — cells/modules completed in Cambodia, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam with Chinese parts circumvent the China AD/CVD orders; duties effective Jun 2024 after the moratorium [S31]. Apr 2025 final AD/CVD on SE-Asia solar reached combined rates up to 3,521%.
- **DOCUMENTED FACT (DOL):** solar-grade polysilicon is mixed/melted/cast into ingots, making Xinjiang-origin tracing "extremely challenging"; Xinjiang ≈ 40–45% of global solar polysilicon; China ~97–99% of wafers — third-country wafer capacity (Indonesia/Laos/India/Middle East) is the weak link [S23].
- **Mechanism note (inference):** polysilicon-to-ingot is the mineral-blending analogue in electronics: an economically unavoidable transformation step that doubles as the laundering step. UFLPA's "wholly or in part" standard [S17] exists precisely because sub-tier inputs cannot be excluded by any document review.

## 7. Pattern extraction: the laundering-step taxonomy

| # | Step | Mechanism | Case anchors |
|---|------|-----------|--------------|
| L1 | **Refining/blending pool** | Fungible output destroys input identity | Perth Mint [S40]; cobalt [S28]; polysilicon [S23] |
| L2 | **Transshipment through non-cooperating hub** | Re-attestation at each border | Gold via UAE/Turkey/Armenia [S24, S35]; GPUs via Malaysia/Vietnam [S29, S30, S33] |
| L3 | **Minor/invisible transformation** | "Substantial transformation" re-draws origin | SE-Asia solar module completion [S31]; diamond polishing [S25] |
| L4 | **Coverage-hole exploitation** | Move volume where the scheme is suspended | ITSCI Masisi [S27]; Myanmar [S27] |
| L5 | **Shell/ownership layering** | Listed party's subsidiary is unlisted | pre-Affiliates-Rule evasion [S03]; Boss Mining Solution [S26] |
| L6 | **Post-hoc relabeling** | Physical marking removed or replaced | chip sanding/re-marking (counterfeit mode) [S53] |

**Key finding (inference):** every step L1–L6 is *lawful or unpoliced in itself*. Laundering is not exotic — it is the ordinary operation of an intermediary layer, absent a record that transformations append to. That absence is the design target for `../05_Origin_Record_Design/`.
