# Origin Evidence — How Origin Is Attested Today

_Covers RQ1 (practice side) + RQ3 (standards side). Every claim cites `[S##]` from `../06_Evidence/Sources.md`. Research pass of 2026-10-06._

## 1. Certificates of origin and customs declarations

Certificates of origin (non-preferential and preferential) are issued on the basis of **declarer's statements and documentary review** — no physical witness is attached. The customs "origin" concept is the substantial-transformation test documented in `../01_Background/Regimes.md` §5 [S25, S31]. OLAF's 2024 caseload (254 new investigations from >1,140 fraud reports; customs-fraud mandate explicitly covering false origin declarations to evade AD/CVD duties) shows the volume of misdeclaration this model generates [S75].

_The evidence model: paper upon paper. A certificate of origin certifies that someone said something to a customs officer._

## 2. OECD Due Diligence Guidance — the five-step framework and its evidence model

The OECD Guidance (3rd ed 2016) supplies the scaffold for effectively every mineral regime [S21]:

1. Strong company management systems
2. Identify and assess risks in the supply chain
3. Design and implement a strategy to respond to identified risks
4. Independent third-party audits of smelter/refiner due-diligence practices
5. Annual reporting

**Evidence model: attestation.** Step 4 audits the *quality of a company's paper system*, not the physical correspondence between a shipment and its declared mine. Annex II risk classes define what must be reported; nothing in the framework binds ore to origin physically. BGR's fingerprinting service exists precisely because the OECD model cannot answer "is this actually from where the tag says" [S36].

## 3. Smelter/auditor programs (RMI/RMAP, LBMA, RJC, IRMA)

- **RMI/RMAP-style smelter audits** and the EU's alignment of 3TG importer duties with the OECD framework [S22] place the audit unit at the smelter — the single point where blending destroys upstream identity anyway.
- **IRMA** third-party mine-site audits (100-point scale; e.g., Mogalakwena IRMA 50, Mar 2025) attest conditions at a named mine **at audit time** — they say nothing about what happens to the ore after it leaves the gate [S47].
- **LBMA good-delivery** refs failed their first public stress test with the Perth Mint dilution/AML findings — refinery accreditation attests process quality, not origin truth [S40].

## 4. Industry schemes for 3TG, cobalt, mica

- **ITSCI** (tag-and-track for 3T in the Great Lakes region): its own statement of Feb 5 2025 admits traceability was suspended in Masisi, North Kivu for almost all of 2024 — ~120 t/month flowing untraced — and Myanmar coverage collapsed after the 2021 coup [S27]. The flagship tag-and-trace scheme documents its own coverage holes.
- **Cobalt**: ASM ore (~20–30% of DRC output) is blended into industrial feeds such that buyer-side separation attempts are "futile" — documented by the Geneva Center/NYU Stern study [S28].
- **Diamonds**: the G7 phased ban (Jan 1 2024 ≥1 ct; Sep 1 2024 ≥0.5 ct) plus the Antwerp verification node is the first regime to bind stones to certificates — but it covers only a size band of rough and relies on declared provenance at intermediary hubs [S25].
- **Blockchain pilots** (Re|Source, Circulor, Tracr) digitize the attestation without changing its epistemics — see `../04_Tracing_Technologies/` §2.

## 5. Where the paper model breaks

Four documented break points (each with a case in `../03_Laundering_Cases/`):

1. **Blending/refining pools** — refinery input from many sources becomes fungible output; provenance is destroyed chemically, not just procedurally (Perth Mint; cobalt ASM blending) [S40, S28].
2. **Coverage holes** — a scheme can only attest what it covers; suspension (ITSCI Masisi) converts tagged supply into untagged supply with no regime signal [S27].
3. **Intermediary re-papering** — lawful transshipment through non-cooperating jurisdictions (UAE/Turkey/Armenia for gold) re-attaches clean origin [S24, S35].
4. **Burden asymmetry** — UFLPA makes importers prove a negative across 6+ tiers of chain [S17]; the EU FLR asks under-resourced authorities to prove forced labour in another jurisdiction [S18]. Both reduce, in practice, to sampling audits of paper.

**Inference:** the paper model does not fail because auditors are careless; it fails because **attestation is the wrong evidence class for a claim that a physical object has a specific origin**. Every documented laundering technique in `../03_Laundering_Cases/` operates by converting a physical claim into a paperwork transaction.
