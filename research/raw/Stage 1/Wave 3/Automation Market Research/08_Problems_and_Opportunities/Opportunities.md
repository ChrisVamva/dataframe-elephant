# Opportunities in automation

## Product opportunities

1. **Automation observability for SMBs** — inventory, owners, failure history, cost, and permission review in one place.
2. **Cross-platform policy layer** — enforce approval, data, and identity rules across Zapier, n8n, RPA, and agents.
3. **Durable agent adapters** — package LangGraph/agent workflows with idempotency, retries, audit, and deployment defaults.
4. **Human review infrastructure** — risk-based approval queues with context, evidence, expiry, and escalation.
5. **Automation cost intelligence** — forecast spend across tasks, actions, tokens, storage, and human work.
6. **Legacy-to-API migration** — detect RPA candidates that should become stable integrations.
7. **Industry-specific automation** — workflows pre-mapped to compliance, terminology, and systems of record.
8. **Agent capability registry** — discover, authenticate, authorize, and evaluate MCP/A2A tools and agents.

## Service opportunities

- Automation audit and inventory.
- Process redesign before tooling.
- Migration from fragile UI bots to APIs.
- Workflow reliability engineering.
- Agent governance and evaluation.
- Fractional automation platform ownership for small companies.

## Opportunity filter

Prefer opportunities where the buyer has a repeated, costly, measurable process; access to reliable data; an identifiable owner; and a safe way to handle exceptions.

Avoid opportunities where the process is rare, ambiguous, politically unowned, or impossible to verify after automation.
