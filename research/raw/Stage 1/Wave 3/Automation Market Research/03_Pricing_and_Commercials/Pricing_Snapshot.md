## Cost model checklist

1. Subscription or platform fee.
2. Tasks, executions, actions, or agent calls.
3. Model/token cost.
4. Connector or premium-app cost.
5. Storage, logs, and retention.
6. Human review and exception-handling labor.
7. Implementation and maintenance.
8. Security, compliance, and support tier.

## Commercial conclusion

The cheapest sticker price is rarely the cheapest total cost. Compare a representative workflow at expected volume, including failures, retries, approval steps, data storage, and maintenance time.
