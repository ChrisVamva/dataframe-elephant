## Research thesis

Automation is not one market. It is a stack of overlapping markets:

1. **Personal and SMB integration** — connect SaaS apps quickly.
2. **Low-code workflow automation / iPaaS** — coordinate APIs, data, approvals, and business rules.
3. **RPA and process automation** — automate desktop/UI and repetitive back-office work.
4. **Developer workflow orchestration** — durable, observable execution for code and services.
5. **AI-agent orchestration** — combine models, tools, state, memory, approvals, and agent-to-agent work.
6. **Automation infrastructure** — queues, schedulers, event buses, identity, secrets, observability, governance.
