- **Academic research** — Thousands of peer-reviewed papers use R for statistical analysis
- **Clinical trials** — Pharmaceutical research and drug development
- **Epidemiology** — Disease modeling and public health research
- **Econometrics** — Economic forecasting and policy analysis
- **Psychometrics** — Psychological testing and measurement
- **Bioinformatics** — Genomics and computational biology (Bioconductor)

- **Shiny** — Interactive web applications for data exploration
