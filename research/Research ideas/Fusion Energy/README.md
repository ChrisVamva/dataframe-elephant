# Fusion Energy — Current State of Research

A structured research workspace for surveying the **current state of fusion energy** (as of October 2026): scientific progress, experimental milestones, commercial landscape, and open challenges.

## Research Questions

- **RQ1** — What is the current state of the art in fusion energy (ignition, Q factors, plasma confinement records)?
- **RQ2** — Which confinement approaches exist (tokamak, stellarator, ICF, alternates), and how do they compare in maturity and prospects?
- **RQ3** — What milestones have the major public and private programs reached (ITER, NIF, JET, private ventures)?
- **RQ4** — What are the principal remaining obstacles (materials, tritium fuel cycle, net electricity, economics)?
- **RQ5** — What are credible timelines for commercial fusion power, and what signals support or contradict them?

## Folder Map

| Folder | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| `00_Index/` | Navigation map, research questions, reading list |
| `01_Background/` | Fusion fundamentals needed to read the literature (physics, fuel cycles, metrics like Q) |
| `02_Approaches/` | One note per confinement approach: tokamak, stellarator, ICF, magnetized target, FRC, Z-pinch |
| `03_State_of_the_Art/` | Current records and milestones; profiles of major public programs and private companies |
| `04_Challenges/` | Open problems: materials, tritium breeding, heat exhaust, economics, regulation |
| `05_Outlook/` | Timelines, expert forecasts, follow-up research agenda |
| `06_Evidence/` | Source register and cumulative findings (every claim traces to a source) |
| `99_Archive/` | Superseded notes and raw clippings kept out of the main structure |

## Conventions

- Notes are Markdown; one topic per note, named `Topic.md`.
- Every factual claim in `01`–`05` folders should carry a source reference into `06_Evidence/Sources.md`.
- Date-stamp findings (`2026-10-02`) so the survey can be refreshed later.
- Superseded material moves to `99_Archive/` — never delete.
