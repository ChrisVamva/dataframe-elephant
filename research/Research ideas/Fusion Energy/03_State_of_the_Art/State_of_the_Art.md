## Major public programs

### ITER (France, 35-nation) [S12] [S13] [S14] [S15] [S46]
- Baseline 2024: research operations ~2033–34, full D–T ~2039, +~€5B cost (total estimates >€40B all-in; no consolidated official figure).
- Assembly accelerating: 6th of 9 sector modules installed Jul 28, 2026, ~6 months ahead of schedule; Europe's first vacuum vessel sector installed Dec 2025.
- Risk flags: staff cuts/union dispute reported Sep 2026 (unverified).

### NIF / US ICF program [S01] [S22]
- Ignition physics proven and repeatable; DOE's $42M IFE hubs (STARFIRE et al., Dec 2023) pursue the missing pieces: efficient lasers, ~Hz rep-rates, mass target manufacturing.

### EUROfusion / EU [S04]
- JET completed its final D–T campaign (69 MJ record) and entered decommissioning Feb 2024; roadmap targets EU-DEMO grid electricity ~2050; DTT (Italy) attacks the divertor problem; European Fusion Industry Platform launched 2024.

### United Kingdom [S18] [S19] [S20]
- Fusion Strategy 2026: >£2.5B over five years, £45M for an AI supercomputer for fusion.
- STEP (spherical tokamak, West Burton): delivery phase 2025–2032; DCO submission by Mar 2029; grid electricity in the 2040s.
- MAST-U: world-first 3D-coil ELM suppression (Oct 2025); Super-X divertor ~10× heat-exhaust reduction.

### China [S16] [S17] [S06] [S07]
- EAST: 1,066 s H-mode (Jan 2025); density-limit breakthrough reported Jan 2026.
- BEST (Hefei): completion targeted end-2027 — burning-plasma demo and Q>1 ambition ahead of CFETR; CRAFT manufacturing/R&D infrastructure in advanced construction.

### Others [S05] [S11] [S21] [S40]
- Korea: KSTAR tungsten divertor, 300 s @ 100M °C goal (2026 target) → K-DEMO.
- Japan: JT-60SA (world's largest operating superconducting tokamak) resumed upgraded ops ~summer 2026; national strategy targets an electricity demo in the 2030s (J-Fusion, FAST prototype).
- US: DOE Fusion Science & Technology Roadmap (Oct 16, 2025) targets commercial deployment by the mid-2030s; milestone-based program ($46M, 8 companies, 2023) [S22] [S23].
