## Landscape synthesis (2025–2026)

- **Tokamaks dominate** public programs and funding (ITER, SPARC/ARC, STEP, BEST) and roughly half the private companies [S31] [S32].
- **Stellarators are the fastest-rising alternative**: W7-X records plus the largest new private rounds (Proxima €411M) and first licences (Type One, Tennessee) [S08] [S41] [S23].
- **ICF holds the only ignition demonstrations** (NIF) but the weakest near-term power-plant story (efficiency, rep-rate, targets) [S01] [S03].
- **Alternates (FRC, MTF, Z-pinch) hold the most aggressive private timelines with the least independent validation** — Helion 2028 PPA being the highest-profile example [S25] [S44].
- IAEA World Fusion Outlook 2025 counts **160+ devices** operational, under construction, or planned worldwide [S32]; FIA counts 56 private companies with $14.24B cumulative investment [S31].
