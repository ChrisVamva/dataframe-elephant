# Inertial Confinement Fusion (ICF / IFE)

_State as of 2026-10-02. Citations: `../06_Evidence/Sources.md`._

## How it works

Lasers (or another driver) compress a millimeter-scale fuel capsule to extreme density and temperature faster than the fuel can disassemble — ignition held together by inertia. Indirect drive converts laser light to X-rays inside a hohlraum (NIF's method); direct drive illuminates the capsule directly (OMEGA, Rochester).

## Maturity & best results

- **The only approach that has demonstrated ignition and target gain > 1.** NIF ignition shots (all verified via LLNL's milestone page) [S01]:
  - Dec 5, 2022 — 3.15 MJ from 2.05 MJ (first ignition; gain ≈ 1.5)
  - Jul 30, 2023 — 3.88 MJ; Feb 12, 2024 — 5.2 MJ (gain ≈ 2.4) [S02]
  - **Apr 7, 2025 — 8.6 MJ from 2.08 MJ, target gain 4.13 (all-time record)**
  - Jun 20, 2026 — 7.9 MJ, gain ≈ 3.8 (11th ignition)
- Repeat capability demonstrated (11 ignitions by mid-2026), but ~1 shot/day cadence [S01].
- Private/IFE-side: Xcimer Energy switched on "Phoenix," billed as the world's largest private laser (Jun 2026), and passed a DOE preconceptual design milestone for its "Athena" plant concept [S43] (company claims). First Light Fusion abandoned its projectile approach (2025), pivoted to the FLARE high-gain IFE concept and tritium-breeding work; financially strained [S47].

## Advantages / disadvantages

- **+** Only demonstrated ignition; no long-pulse materials/divertor problem; targets mass-producible in principle.
- **−** NIF laser wall-plug efficiency ~0.5% → engineering gain ~0.02; needs ~100× total gain for a plant; rep-rate and target-fabrication cost unsolved; no IFE power-plant design demonstrated [S01] [S03].

## Representative devices

NIF (LLNL), OMEGA (Rochester), Z/MagLIF at Sandia (hybrid, see Z-Pinch.md), LaserNetUS facilities; private: Xcimer, (First Light Fusion — repurposed).
