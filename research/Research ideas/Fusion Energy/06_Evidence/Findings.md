# Findings Log

_Cumulative, date-stamped findings. One bullet per finding, each citing a Source Register ID (`Sources.md`). Raw material for the final synthesis._

## 2026-10-02

### Records & state of the art (RQ1)

- NIF has achieved ignition 11 times as of mid-2026; all-time record is 8.6 MJ fusion yield from 2.08 MJ laser energy (target gain 4.13) on Apr 7, 2025; latest 7.9 MJ / gain ≈3.8 on Jun 20, 2026. This is *target gain* — wall-plug gain remains ~0.008 (lasers draw ~300+ MJ electricity per shot, ~1 shot/day) [S01] [S03].
- JET's final D–T campaign (shot Oct 3, 2023, announced Feb 8, 2024) set the tokamak fusion-energy record: 69 MJ over ~5.2 s; JET then ceased operation and entered decommissioning Feb 2024. Record still stood as of Oct 2026 [S04].
- EAST sustained H-mode plasma for 1,066 s on Jan 20, 2025 (prior record 403 s, 2023); in Jan 2026 ASIPP reported a route to exceed the Greenwald density limit [S06] [S07].
- KSTAR's verified 100-million-°C duration record is 48 s (2024 campaign, first with tungsten divertor); a reported 102 s (Jun 2026) is disputed — no KFE primary release [S05].
- Wendelstein 7-X set a stellarator triple-product record with 43 s of high-performance plasma, and a world-record 1.8 GJ plasma energy turnover over 360 s (May 22, 2025) [S08] [S09] [S10].
- JT-60SA (world's largest operating superconducting tokamak) reached first plasma Oct 2023, resumed upgraded operations ~summer 2026 with tungsten divertor components [S11] [S21].

### Public programs (RQ3)

- ITER Baseline 2024: research operations ~2033–34, full D–T ~2039, +~€5B cost; sixth of nine sector modules installed Jul 28, 2026, ~6 months ahead of schedule [S12] [S13] [S14] [S15].
- China's BEST tokamak targets completion end-2027, aiming at burning plasma and first fusion power generation demo; CRAFT facility in advanced construction [S16] [S17].
- UK: Fusion Strategy 2026 commits >£2.5B over five years; STEP (spherical tokamak, West Burton) targets grid electricity in the 2040s, DCO submission by Mar 2029 [S18] [S19].
- US DOE released its Fusion Science & Technology Roadmap Oct 16, 2025 targeting commercial deployment by the mid-2030s; milestone-based program awarded $46M to 8 companies (2023) [S22] [S23].
- IAEA's first World Fusion Outlook (Oct 14, 2025) counts 160+ fusion devices operational/under construction/planned worldwide [S32].

### Private sector (RQ3, RQ5)

- Private fusion investment hit a record $4.48B in the 12 months to Jul 2026 ($14.24B cumulative, 56 companies) [S31]; prior year: $2.64B, $9.77B cumulative [S30].
- CFS raised $863M Series B2 (Aug 2025, investors incl. Nvidia and Google); SPARC >75% assembled but third-party analysis suggests first plasma may slip to 2027; ARC (400 MWe, Virginia) targets early 2030s with Google (200 MW, Jun 2025) and Eni (>$1B, Sep 2025) offtakes [S24] [S50].
- Helion announced Polaris as first private machine running on D–T fuel at ~150M °C (Feb 2026, company claim); the Microsoft 50 MW / 2028 PPA remains the highest-risk single date — no public net-energy gain yet [S25] [S48] [S44].
- Consolidation into public markets: General Fusion listed on Nasdaq (GFUZ, Jul 2026) after a 2025 funding crisis (~25% layoffs); TAE announced a >$6B all-stock merger with Trump Media (Dec 2025, pending) [S29] [S26].
- Proxima Fusion (stellarator) raised €411M (Jul/Sep 2026), Europe's largest private fusion round [S41].

### Challenges (RQ4)

- No integrated tritium-breeding blanket has ever operated in a fusion neutron environment; world civilian tritium stock is tens of kg, mostly from CANDU reactors [S49] [S12].
- Lithium-6 enrichment is a chokepoint: only Russia and China actively produce enriched Li-6 [S33].
- IFMIF-DONES materials facility is in construction (main accelerator building works underway 2026); DEMO-relevant materials data cannot exist before the mid-2030s at best.
- Regulation is de-risking: US NRC regulates fusion under the Part 30 byproduct-materials framework (2023, rulemaking completing ~2027); UK regulates fusion outside nuclear site licensing (Energy Act 2023); Canada consulting (DIS-25-01, Aug 2025); Tennessee issued the first US state fusion license (Type One Energy, Sep 2026) [S36] [S37] [S38] [S23].

### Synthesis

- Physics milestones are landing before economics: Q>1 in a tokamak is plausibly 2026–2028 (SPARC, BEST), but credible first-grid-power dates cluster late-2030s/2040s; material grid contribution 2050+. Binding constraints are engineering (tritium, materials, exhaust, recirculating power), not physics.
