# Research Intake Form

## Role

<!-- include: lib/role_research_agent.md -->

Fix what the subject is before any search begins. The authoritative rules and the mandatory question set live in `Rules and Regulations/Protocols/ResearchIntake.md`; this form is their downstream mirror. Ask, record, and stop for confirmation — do not start searching.

## How to run it

- Ask one round of at most four questions, using the interactive question tool.
- Give every question a recommended default plus an explicit "use your default" choice.
- Treat a default as an assumption, not as the requester's answer.
- Re-ask a vague answer once with concrete options; a category label is not a scope.
- Ask a second round of at most four questions only when the first round leaves a material gap.
- Restate the agreed scope in one paragraph and wait for confirmation before searching.

## Round 1 — subject and scope

1. Subject and the one-sentence research question.
2. In scope: which entities, tiers, or populations are included?
3. Out of scope: what is explicitly excluded?
4. Boundary: which geography, time period, and unit of comparison apply?

## Round 2 — evidence and output (only if a material gap remains)

5. Intended output: article, database, decision aid, or brief?
6. Evidence bar: minimum acceptable evidence class, and which source tiers count?
7. Comparators: which alternatives or counterexamples must be covered?
8. Stop criteria: what makes the survey complete?

## Record

Capture the answers and the declared scope in the subject's intake record beside its wave index, then confirm before searching. The declared scope must satisfy `RE:Gate A`.
